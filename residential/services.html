Residential painting questions

How do you protect my furniture and floors during interior painting?

Careful attention to detail is always given to the protection of all furnishings and accessories while we work in your home. Floors, furniture and fixtures are covered before any prep or paint begins.

What if there is rotten wood on my house?

Rotten wood can be patched or replaced with new wood, producing the look of new construction. Our staff carpenter handles repairs during the project so progress does not slow down and deadlines are met.

Do you remove wallpaper and repair drywall before painting?

Yes. We handle wallpaper removal, wood and plaster repair and drywall surface preparation so the new paint goes onto a sound, smooth surface.
