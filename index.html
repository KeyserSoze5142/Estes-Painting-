Questions we hear most

Quick answers about hiring a painting contractor in Birmingham.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Birmingham, AL?

Every home is different, so Estes Painting prices each project after a free on-site visit. The estimate covers surface preparation, carpentry repairs if any wood is rotten, materials and labor, with no obligation. Call (205) 918-8982 or request an estimate online.

Does Estes Painting do both interior and exterior painting?

Yes. Our residential crews handle interior painting, exterior painting, painted brick and stucco, ceilings, cabinets and trim, plus the carpentry repairs and surface preparation a lasting paint job needs.

Is Estes Painting licensed and insured?

Yes. Estes Painting Company is licensed, carries the maximum liability insurance, and runs background checks and drug screens on every painter.

What areas does Estes Painting serve?

We serve greater Birmingham, Alabama, including Greystone, Homewood, Hoover, Inverness, Mountain Brook and Vestavia Hills. Our Hoover office is at 2256 Rocky Ridge Rd #102.

Can Estes Painting handle large commercial buildings?

Yes. Our commercial crews paint office buildings, apartment complexes, condominium and townhouse associations, schools, churches, restaurants, retail locations and warehouses, with equipment that includes scaffolding, bucket trucks and a crane for taller structures.

Do you offer services besides painting?

Yes. We also provide carpentry and wood rot repair, deck building, replacement and staining, window and door replacement, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, power washing, faux finishing and garage floor coatings.
