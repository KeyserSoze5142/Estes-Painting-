Deck questions

Do you build new decks or just repair them?

Both. We install all types and styles of wooden decks, replace existing decks, and repair and renovate decks, including stains and water protectants.

Should I choose wood or Trex composite decking?

Be sure to ask about composite decking systems such as Trex. While the initial cost may be higher, you save money in the long run on the hours and dollars spent maintaining a traditional wooden deck.

Do you stain and seal decks?

Yes. We apply stains and water protectants for long-lasting beauty, and we repair boards, rails and stairs before sealing.

Can you add a roof, screen room or stairs to my deck?

Yes. Our carpenters have added roofs, screened rooms, stair platforms, cable railings and LED lighting to decks across greater Birmingham. See the gallery for examples.
