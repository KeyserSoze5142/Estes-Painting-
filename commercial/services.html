We have extensive exterior painting experience as well as expertise in the surface preparation required prior to applying a successful coat of paint.

Commercial painting questions

What types of commercial buildings does Estes Painting work on?

Offices, churches, schools, nursing homes, apartment complexes, condominium and townhouse associations, restaurants, retail locations and warehouses throughout greater Birmingham.

Can you paint while our business stays open?

Yes. Our commercial clients attest to our crews' ability to accomplish the work with a minimum of disruption to their day-to-day operation, and we work around your schedule.

Do you have equipment for tall buildings?

Yes. We keep a large assortment of professional equipment, including scaffolding, industrial material elevators, bucket trucks and a crane for taller structures.

Are your commercial painters screened?

Yes. We run background checks and drug screens on all of our painters, and the company carries maximum liability insurance.

Do you handle wallpaper removal and drywall repair in commercial interiors?

Yes. We have painted the interiors of hundreds of professional buildings, churches and schools and handle all interior surface issues, including wallpaper removal and drywall repair.
